This is a timed coding assessment sent to you by your H10 Capital recruiter. You will get one problem in your chosen language — either the 15-minute Screen or the 40-minute Deep problem. Choose the tier your recruiter specified. When you finish, this page creates a results summary for your recruiter at tuan@h10capital.com. Rules — please read
  • Complete it on your own — no AI assistants, no code lookup, no help from another person.
  • Solutions must be typed in the editor — pasting is disabled.
  • Leaving this page during the test is recorded and reported with your results.
  • Think in code, not perfection: a complete, simple solution beats an unfinished clever one.
  • Your code and results are not stored on this site. When you finish, your results are sent to your recruiter at tuan@h10capital.com automatically; you can also copy or email the summary yourself as a backup.
  • How scoring works: for Python and JavaScript, the checks run entirely in your browser. For C++, C#, and Java, your code is sent to a public code-execution service (Piston, with Judge0 as a fallback) to compile and run the checks; only your code is sent — your name and email are never sent to that service.
  • The timer starts when you press Start assessment and cannot be paused. If time runs out, the editor locks and your results are generated as they stand.

By starting, you confirm you will complete this on your own, without AI assistance or code lookup. Pasting is disabled and page departures are recorded.
